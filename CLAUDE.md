# Rules for working on heroes-capture

These are the user's standing rules. They apply to every task, every session.

1. **Look in the game data first, before deciding to measure.** Sizes, timings, animation
   lengths, bounds, scales, positions: find the field in the game's data (m3 files, catalogs,
   the map's own data) and read it. When the data has been checked and gives a poor answer,
   measuring is fine (and better, if it gives better results); say what was searched.
2. **Look things up before guessing.** Search posts and documentation, especially the StarCraft II
   modding material out there (Heroes runs on the StarCraft II engine: actors and their messages,
   the m3 format, data fields, trigger functions).
3. **Deliver exactly what was asked.** Nothing more, nothing less.
