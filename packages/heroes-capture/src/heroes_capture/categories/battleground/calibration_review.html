Calibration review

What the calibration settled by itself is summed up below. The rest needs your eye. Submit sends your answers back, and the render is prepared from them.

Fall timing

Pick the frame where the rubble looks right for the map: debris landed, smoke and dust still there. Each kind of structure, on each team, was filmed falling, a frame every quarter second. The render shoots that kind's rubble at the moment you pick.

Standing timing

Pick the frame where the structure looks right standing: fully built, its fires and lights on. Each type was restarted and filmed through its standing animation. The render restarts every structure of the type and pauses it at the moment you pick, so it looks the same every run.

Copy spots to check

Does the copy look like the structure? A structure's rubble is shot from a copy of it standing on another spot. These copies only just passed the automatic check. Reject one and that structure falls by itself in the render.

Team colour

Does this kind of structure change colour with its team (blue for Order, red for Chaos)? When the side was switched, some structures of the kind seemed to change and some didn't, so the calibration couldn't tell. Click a picture to see it at full size.
